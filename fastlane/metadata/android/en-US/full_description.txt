Iron Will is a private, easy-to-use streak and habit tracker that helps you stay consistent and build better habits.

Track your progress in real time, record setbacks, set goals, and celebrate each milestone as your streak grows.

KEY FEATURES

- Live streak counter with days, hours, minutes, and seconds
- Multiple timers for tracking different habits
- Progressive goals from 3 days up to 1 year
- Milestone badges and dynamic ranks
- Streak history with relapse dates and personal notes
- Statistics for current, best, and lifetime progress
- Rotating motivational quotes
- Optional 4-digit PIN protection
- Nine launcher icons and neutral disguise names
- Encrypted export and import for secure backups
- Support for nine languages
- Local-first storage with no account required
